❓ Troubleshooting

Here are answers to common questions. If you need more help, visit the support section below.

The QR code doesn’t work

Make sure your phone and computer are connected to the internet. Update your Telegram app to the latest version. If the QR code expires, restart the application to generate a new one.

Downloads are slow

Check your internet connection. Try pausing other downloads or streaming. The multi-connection feature works best on stable networks. You can also restart the application.

Windows shows a security warning

This can happen with new applications. Click “More info” and then “Run anyway” if you trust the source. Always download from the official link provided on this page.

Files are not saving

Make sure you have enough free disk space and that the download folder is not write-protected. Try choosing a different folder, such as your Desktop or Documents.
